# Nakaja Brand Voice Skill

## Brand Personality

Nakaja Bikes speaks with Humanized Hustle.

The tone is:

- professional
- rugged
- direct
- confident
- practical
- encouraging
- Nairobi-market aware

The audience is not looking for luxury fluff. They want a bike that can work, earn, move, and survive daily use.

## Buyer Mindset

Nakaja buyers ask:

- How much deposit?
- How much daily?
- How much weekly?
- Is the bike strong?
- Can it handle work?
- Where is the showroom?
- Can I talk on WhatsApp?
- Is financing available?
- Can I trust this dealer?

Copy must answer these quickly.

## Approved Positioning

Use:

- Authorized UM Motorbikes Dealer
- Nairobi motorcycle dealer
- Industrial Area showroom
- Cash and financing options
- Built for daily riders
- Built for boda boda work
- Built for city movement
- Talk to Nakaja on WhatsApp
