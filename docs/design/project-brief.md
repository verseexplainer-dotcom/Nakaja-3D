# Project Brief: Nakaja Bikes - "Cinematic Velocity"

## 1. Project Overview
Nakaja Bikes is a premium Kenyan motorcycle dealership specializing in high-performance machinery built for rugged local terrains. The digital experience must bridge the gap between aspirational Italian racing aesthetics (Ducati/MV Agusta) and the uncompromising reliability required for Kenyan roads.

## 2. Strategic Objectives
- **High-Fidelity Branding:** Establish Nakaja as the premier authority for luxury and performance motorcycles in Kenya.
- **Conversion-First Design:** Prioritize WhatsApp-based inquiries and service bookings as the primary lead generation channel.
- **Educational Value:** Provide deep technical specifications and transparent financing options to build trust with high-intent buyers.

## 3. Visual Identity & Design System
**Theme Name:** Cinematic Velocity
- **Aesthetic:** Dark cinematic dealership, metallic UI, high-contrast lighting.
- **Color Palette:**
    - Primary: Racing Red (#E31837) for action and urgency.
    - Background: Obsidian/Charcoal (#131313) for a premium "dark room" feel.
    - Accents: Metallic silver and subtle borders for industrial precision.
- **Typography:** Space Grotesk (Industrial, precision-engineered feel).
- **Device Strategy:** Mobile-first, optimized for fast browsing on-the-go.

## 4. Key Features & User Flows
### A. The Digital Showroom
- High-impact hero sections with "New Arrival" and "Best Seller" badges.
- Smooth transitions between categories (Sport, Adventure, Commuter).
- Quick-view pricing in KES.

### B. Technical Dominance (Bike Specs)
- Detailed performance metrics (BHP, Torque, Efficiency).
- Interactive financing calculator with monthly installment projections.
- Direct "Apply for Financing" and "Negotiate on WhatsApp" CTAs.

### C. Precision Maintenance (Service & Support)
- One-tap "Book a Service" workflow.
- Genuine Parts catalog browsing.
- 24/7 Emergency Roadside Assistance integration (Call/WhatsApp).

### D. Inventory Management
- Categorized fleet browsing with specification snapshots.
- Status indicators (New Release, Best Seller).

## 5. Technical Requirements
- **Responsive Layout:** Optimized for mobile viewports with bottom navigation for easy thumb-reach.
- **Performance:** Lightweight UI components to ensure fast loading over mobile data.
- **Integration:** Seamless deep-linking to WhatsApp and Phone dialers.

## 6. Success Metrics
- Increase in WhatsApp inquiry volume.
- User engagement depth on technical specification pages.
- Lead conversion rate from the financing calculator.
